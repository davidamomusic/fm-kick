FM-KICK · Cheat sheet

FM-KICK at a glance

For FM-KICK 0.5.1 on the M-VAVE FM-1

A kick drum synthesizer with a sequencer of up to 32 steps: seven kinds of kick and 48 parameters, p-locks of any parameter on any step, step conditions and chance, nine generators, four LFOs, live and final effects with a hall reverb, three pattern banks, four projects and six themes.

Install FM-KICK 0.5.1 Source code Print

FM-KICK is beta software. Use it at your own risk: it can have bugs and fail, and installing it or restoring a backup writes to the FM-1's flash. Save what matters first and never unplug the USB cable while it writes. BUTTON a button, key or encoder of the FM-1  ·  tap, hold, turn  ·  A + B hold A while you use B  ·  K1-K4 are the four knobs, in the order of the screen footer  ·  the white keys are the steps, the black keys play the kick

Getting around

SEQ ARP EDIT LFO FX GLO
The modes. A press goes to the mode, on the page you left; pressed again, the next page (round). ENV does nothing for now.
SELECT turn
The page inside the mode.
KNOB 1-KNOB 4
The four parameters named at the foot of the screen. Each click shows the new value big for half a second; a knob of a few choices moves to the next one.
OCT- + any encoder
Faster.
PLAY
Start or stop.
SEL
Steps 1-16 or 17-32 on the white keys.
SAVE
Save now. It also saves by itself, stopped, 1.5 s after a change.
HOME
The boxing glove: it punches with every hit.

Steps

STEP KEY tap
The 16 white keys are the steps: off, on, accent.
ALGORITHM turn
On SEQ and the EDIT pages: picks a step (its key blinks). Stopped, you hear it if it has a trig. On ARP the generator, on FX the effect the knobs edit, on PATTERNS the pattern.
STEP KEY hold + knobs on ARP
The step's pitch (-24..+24), ratchet (x1-x4), slide and reversed sweep.
STEP KEY hold + knobs on EDIT or SEQ
A p-lock of that parameter on the step.
OCT+ hold + knobs on EDIT
A p-lock on the step ALGORITHM picked.
STEP KEY hold + PRESETS
Another kick on that step (a p-lock), or back to the pattern's.
STEP KEY hold + REC
The step's p-locks cleared.
P-locks
Up to 16 on a step, 74 in a pattern. On EDIT, each page marks the steps with locks of its own four parameters.
OCT- + STEP KEY hold on SEQ
Repeat: that many steps (key 1 = one, 16 = sixteen) round and round while held; let go and it carries on.

Sequencer pages

SEQ KICK
The picked step's TYPE, TUNE, ATTACK and DECAY, as p-locks.
SEQ KICK 2
K1 COND: 1:2 ... 4:4 (the X-th pass of every Y), 1ST, !1ST, PRE, !PRE. K2 PROB, the step's chance (5% a click, 1% with OCT-).
ARP GENERATE
ALGORITHM picks MANUAL, EUCLID, POLY, METRIC, SYNCOPA, BOUNCE, MIRROR, FRACTAL, BITS or ROLLS; the knobs shape it. OCT- + SAVE prints its steps into the TRIG lane.
ARP TRIG
LENGTH (1-32), DIVISION, DIRECTION (FWD, REV, PING, RAND, WANDER), NUDGE (the whole sequence a step a click, locks with it).
ARP FEEL
TEMPO, PROB, HUMANIZE, SWING.
LFO
Four LFOs; each press the next one, SELECT its two pages. Each moves one parameter: length, division, direction, shape (MANUAL, RAMP UP, RAMP DN, TRI, SINE, SQUARE, RANDOM, MEANDER), depth, smoothing, seed.

Recording

REC
Recording on or off. The 27 keys become a keyboard (C4 = the kick's note); keys and MIDI notes go into the steps: playing, the nearest step; stopped, the selected step and then the next.
REC on + knobs on SEQ or EDIT
The knobs recorded as p-locks. Playing: on the step of the kick sounding, carrying on from where the knob stood (a sweep over several kicks). Stopped: on the step REC wrote last. OCT- held: fast.
OCT- + REC
LISTEN: the keys play the kick, nothing is recorded.
OCT+ + REC
Metronome on or off.
Black keys
Play the kick a semitone apart (OCT+ held: accented).

The kick (EDIT)

12 pages, then STORE
KICK (TYPE, TUNE, DECAY, LEVEL), CHARACTER, PITCH, SWEEP, AMP, CLICK, SUB, NOISE, DIST, FILTER, EQ, OUTPUT: 48 parameters.
TYPE
808, 909, 606, ANALOG, FM, WSHAPER, THUNDER; each has its own four CHARACTER knobs.
STORE
32 user kicks with a name of 8 letters: K1 slot, K2 cursor, K3 letter, K4 right saves, left loads. 18 factory kicks.
PRESETS turn
The pattern's kick from the presets.
OCT- + GLO on EDIT
The kick moved a little.
OCT+ + GLO on EDIT
A new kick at random; the steps stay.

Effects (FX)

LIVE: STEP KEY hold
On only while held: DISTORT, CRUSH, FILTER, RING MOD, CHORUS, PHASER, FLANGER, DELAY, REVERB, STUTTER, GATE, WIDTH. The knobs set the last one played.
OCT- + STEP KEY on LIVE
That effect left on, until OCT- + the key again.
OCT+ + STEP KEY
That effect picked for the knobs, nothing played.
OCT+ alone
Every effect off.
FINAL: keys 1-5
The master chain on or off: TRANSIENT, COMP, EQ, DISTORTION, LIMITER.
DELAY
TIME, FEEDBACK, MIX, FILTER (middle open, left low-pass, right high-pass, on the echoes).
REVERB
A hall: DECAY (0.6-8 s), DAMP, MIX, PRE (0-100 ms). Below 90 Hz stays dry.

Random and reset

OCT- + GLO
Random kick parameters and trigs (p-locks too). Never the tempo, the LFOs or the effects. Any random keeps CLICK's LEVEL at 4 or less and no LFO on CLICK.
OCT+ + GLO
The same, and the four LFOs.
OCT- + LFO
The LFOs at random, gently.
OCT+ + LFO
The LFOs at random, wildly.
OCT- + HOME
The page you are on back to its start (on SEQ, the sequence cleared too).
OCT+ + HOME
The whole pattern as new (other patterns, user kicks and settings stay).

Patterns and projects (GLO)

PATTERNS
Black keys 1-3: banks A, B, C (shown; the pattern playing goes on). White keys: the 16 patterns; playing, a pick starts at the end of the bar. ACTION: PLAY, COPY, PASTE, CLEAR, INIT, RANDOM; DO IT does it.
PROJECTS
4 slots: the 3 banks, tempo and effects. K1 slot, K2 LOAD, SAVE or CLEAR, K4 DO IT (turn twice within 3 s). Stopped only.
Other pages
MIDI, THEMES (6 themes: EMBER, NIGHT, ICE, ACID, MONO, PAPER; dials or bars, ring or grid), POPUPS (solid or clear), AUDIO (USB, jack, speaker), SYSTEM (load, xruns, flash, version, cores).

Computer and MIDI

MIDI
In: any note plays the kick (36 = its own tune). Out: each hit as a note, and the clock. CLOCK = MIDI follows an incoming clock.
Screen mirror
The screen and panel over USB (Chrome or Edge): Render Audio to WAV, projects and backups.
Play in the browser
The firmware itself, running in a web page.
Leave FM-KICK
Install another firmware with The Firmware Hub or M-UPGRADE. Identity: FM-1_222.
